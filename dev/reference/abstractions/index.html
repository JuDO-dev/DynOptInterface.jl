Abstractions

The following are sub-types of AbstractDynamicFunction:

  • AbstractAlgebraicFunction
    • $t_i$ DomainIndex
    • $t_i \mapsto y_j(t_i)$ DynamicVariableIndex
    • $t_i \mapsto a(y(t_i), t_i, x)$ NonlinearAlgebraicFunction
  • AbstractDifferentialFunction
    • $t_i \mapsto \dot{y}_j(t_i)$ DynamicVariableDerivative
    • $t_i \mapsto \dot{y}_j(t_i) - a(y(t_i), t_i, x)$ ExplicitDifferentialFunction
    • $t_i \mapsto r(\dot{y}(t_i), y(t_i), t_i, x)$ NonlinearDifferentialFunction
  • AbstractBoundaryFunction
    • $a(y(t_i^0), t_i^0, x)$ Initial
    • $a(y(t_i^f), t_i^f, x)$ Final
    • $b(y(t^0), y(t^f), t^0, t^f, x)$ NonlinearBoundaryFunction
  • $\int_{t_i^0}^{t_i^f} a(y(t_i), t_i, x) \mathrm{d}t_i$ IntegralFunction
  • $b(y(t_i^0), y(t_i^f), t_i^0, t_i^f, x) + \int_{t_i^0}^{t_i^f} a(y(t_i), t_i, x) \mathrm{d}t_i$ BolzaFunction
